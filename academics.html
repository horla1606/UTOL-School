Our Curriculum

Learning That Grows With Your Child

At UTOL Schools, we believe that a truly great education is about so much more than passing tests. From the moment a child joins our Creche to the day they graduate from Year 5, we offer a rich, balanced and joyful curriculum that develops the whole child — academically, creatively, socially and emotionally. Every lesson, every activity and every interaction is carefully designed to spark curiosity, build confidence and lay a rock-solid foundation for future learning.

Our curriculum blends the very best of the Nigerian National Primary School Curriculum with forward-thinking approaches drawn from leading international school systems. We combine strong core academics — English, Mathematics and Science — with creative subjects, physical education and character-building activities, so that every child who leaves UTOL is not just academically ready for the next stage, but truly ready for life.

How We Teach

Our Teaching Philosophy

Three principles guide every lesson at UTOL Schools — ensuring every child is engaged, supported and celebrated throughout their learning journey.

1
🧠

Child-Centred Learning

We understand that every child is beautifully unique. Our teachers take the time to truly know each pupil — their strengths, their interests, the way they learn best — and design lessons that meet them exactly where they are. There is no one-size-fits-all approach at UTOL; every child receives learning that is personal, purposeful and paced just right for them.

✨ Every child is seen, known and valued in our classrooms.
2
🔧

Hands-On Activities

We firmly believe that children learn best when they are actively doing, exploring and experimenting — not just listening or copying from a board. From science experiments and building projects to drama, art and outdoor learning, our lessons are designed to make concepts come alive through experience. When children are engaged with their hands, their minds follow.

🎯 Learning happens best when children are having fun doing it.
3
📈

Continuous Assessment

We never wait until the end of term to find out how a child is doing. Our teachers use continuous, formative assessment throughout every week — tracking progress, identifying gaps and celebrating growth as it happens. Parents are kept fully informed through regular updates and our online portal, so there are never any surprises and every child always gets the support they need, when they need it.

📋 Progress is tracked daily, not just at exam time.
