📄 Admissions

When does the admissions process open for 2025/2026?
+

Admissions for the 2025/2026 academic year are now open. We recommend applying as early as possible as spaces fill up quickly, especially in popular year groups. Applications close once each class is full.

What is the minimum age for Nursery 1 admission?
+

Children must be at least 2 years old to join Nursery 1. We also accept children from 18 months into our Toddler/Creche programme. For Primary 1, children should be 6 years old by September 1st of the academic year.

Is there an entrance examination?
+

Yes. All applicants for Primary 1 and above sit a brief, age-appropriate academic assessment in English and Mathematics. Early Years (Nursery/KG) applicants complete a developmental readiness observation rather than a formal test. The assessments are designed to be stress-free and child-friendly.

Can my child join mid-term or mid-year?
+

Yes, we do accept mid-year enrolments subject to available spaces. Contact our admissions office to check current availability. We will assess your child and provide a seamless transition plan to help them settle in quickly.

How long does the admissions process take?
+

From submitting the application to receiving an offer letter typically takes 7–10 working days. This includes the assessment, interview and internal review. We will keep you informed at every step via email and phone.

💰 Fees & Payments

How are school fees structured?
+

School fees are charged termly (3 terms per academic year). They cover tuition, textbooks, learning materials, use of facilities and standard school activities. A detailed fee schedule will be provided at the point of offer. Additional costs may apply for uniform, optional trips and co-curricular clubs.

Are there sibling discounts available?
+

Yes! We offer a sibling discount of 10% on fees for the second child and 15% for a third or subsequent child enrolled simultaneously. Please mention this at the time of application or contact our finance office for details.

What payment methods are accepted?
+

We accept bank transfers, online payments via our parent portal, and cheques made payable to "UTOL Schools Ltd". Cash payments are accepted at the school's finance office. All receipts are issued digitally via the parent portal.

Is there a scholarship or bursary programme?
+

Yes. UTOL Schools offers merit-based scholarships for academically exceptional students and need-based bursaries for families experiencing financial difficulty. Applications for the scholarship programme open in January each year. Contact the principal's office for more information.

🎓 Academic & Curriculum

What curriculum does UTOL follow?
+

We follow the Nigerian National Primary School Curriculum as approved by NERDC (Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council), enriched with elements of the British curriculum and international best practices. This prepares our students for both local Common Entrance and international assessments.

How many students are in each class?
+

We maintain a maximum of 25 students per class for Early Years and 30 for Primary classes. This ensures every child receives adequate attention. We also have teaching assistants in Early Years and Lower Primary to provide additional support.

How is homework managed?
+

Homework is assigned age-appropriately — minimal in Early Years, building progressively through the primary years. Homework schedules and assignments are shared on the parent portal. We do not support excessive homework that detracts from family time and play.

Does UTOL have special needs or learning support provision?
+

Yes. We have a dedicated Learning Support team that works with children who have additional learning needs, including dyslexia, ADHD and speech/language difficulties. We work closely with parents and external specialists to provide Individual Education Plans (IEPs) where required.

🏠 School Life & Safety

What are the school hours?
+

School runs from 7:30am to 3:30pm, Monday to Friday. Gates open at 7:00am for early drop-off. An after-school care programme is available from 3:30pm – 6:00pm for an additional fee. Contact the admin office to enrol your child in after-school care.

What is the school's safeguarding policy?
+

Child safety and wellbeing are our highest priority. All staff are background-checked and receive mandatory safeguarding training. We have a Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) and a clear reporting protocol. Our full safeguarding policy is available on request from the school office.

Is there a school bus / transport service?
+

Yes, we operate a school bus service covering several routes across Lagos. The service is GPS-tracked and supervised by trained attendants. Bus routes, timetables and fees are available from the admin office or via the parent portal.

What is the uniform policy?
+

UTOL has a compulsory school uniform consisting of navy blue trousers/skirt, a white polo shirt with the UTOL logo, and black shoes. A sports kit and cultural day attire are also required. Uniforms can be purchased at the school's uniform shop or from approved vendors. Full details are on the website's School Life section.

💻 Parent & Student Portal

How do I access the parent portal?
+

The parent portal is accessible at the "Portal Login" link in the top navigation. Your login credentials (email and temporary password) are sent to your registered email address upon enrolment. If you haven't received them or need a password reset, contact IT support at portal@utolschools.edu.ng.

What can I see on the parent portal?
+

The parent portal gives you access to your child's: academic results and report cards, attendance records, timetable, school announcements, fee statements and payment history, messages from teachers, and upcoming events. It is updated in real time by school staff.

Can I contact my child's teacher through the portal?
+

Yes. The portal has a built-in messaging system that allows parents to send messages directly to class teachers and other staff. All messages are monitored and responded to during school hours. For urgent matters, please call the school directly.

Still Have Questions?

Our admissions and support team are happy to help. Reach out via phone, email, or in person during school hours.
