Our Story

✨ “…in service for the best”

UTOL Schools was born in 2006 out of a simple but powerful belief — that every child in Arepo and beyond deserves access to a warm, safe and academically excellent learning environment from the very earliest age. What started as a small, passionate community of educators with a shared dream has grown into one of Ogun State's most trusted and beloved primary schools, serving hundreds of families across Creche, Kindergarten and Year 1 through Year 5.

Since opening our doors, we have remained true to our founding values: putting children first, building lasting partnerships with families and continuously striving to raise the standard of early childhood and primary education. Every classroom, every lesson and every smile we see on our pupils' faces is a reminder of why we started. We are not just a school — we are a family, and we are deeply proud to be “in service for the best” each and every day.

Why Choose Us

What Makes UTOL Special

Four reasons why hundreds of Arepo families trust UTOL Schools with the most precious thing in their lives — their children.

🧠

Child-Centred Learning

We believe every child learns differently. Our teachers design lessons that meet pupils where they are, nurture individual strengths and gently challenge every learner to grow. Small class sizes ensure no child is ever overlooked or left behind.

🏠

Safe, Welcoming Environment

Our campus at 7/9 Jemimat Laalu Street is a secure, clean and beautifully maintained environment where children feel happy and at home from day one. Safeguarding is at the heart of everything we do — every staff member is trained and vetted.

🏆

Proven Academic Excellence

Since 2006, our pupils have consistently achieved outstanding results. Our Year 5 leavers are well-prepared for secondary school, with strong foundations in English, Mathematics, Science and ICT — as well as the confidence to excel beyond the classroom.

👥

A True Family Partnership

We work hand in hand with parents and guardians every step of the way. Through regular updates, our online portal and an open-door policy, we make sure you are always informed, involved and celebrated as a vital partner in your child's education.
