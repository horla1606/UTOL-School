Admissions

Enrolment for 2025/2026 is now open. Join the UTOL family today.

How to Apply

4 Simple Steps to Enrolment

Our admissions process is straightforward and designed to be stress-free for families.

1

Submit Application

Complete the online application form below or visit our admissions office to collect a physical form.

2

Assessment & Interview

Your child will be invited for a brief age-appropriate assessment and a family interview with our admissions team.

3

Offer of Placement

Successful applicants will receive an offer letter via email within 5–7 working days of the assessment.

4

Acceptance & Enrolment

Accept the offer, pay the acceptance fee, submit required documents and your child's place is confirmed!

What You'll Need

Please have the following documents ready when completing your application.

  • ✓Child's original birth certificate (+ photocopy)
  • ✓2 recent passport photographs of the child
  • ✓Previous school report / transfer certificate (if applicable)
  • ✓Immunisation records
  • ✓Parent / guardian valid ID (2 copies)
  • ✓Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement)
  • ✓Completed application form
